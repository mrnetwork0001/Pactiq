TITLE
Pactiq: stake on a habit, get every kept day back | QIE Blockchain Hackathon 3.0

DESCRIPTION
Quitting a habit costs nothing, so most of us quit. Pactiq puts a price on the promise.

Sign a pact, lock a small daily stake in QIE or QUSDC, and prove each day with a photo and a one-time code. An AI witness checks the photo, the server signs the day, and the contract accepts it once. Kept days are refunded. Missed days fund a bonus pool for the people who finish.

Built on QIE Mainnet, with QIE Wallet, QUSDC, QIEDEX, QIE ID and the QIE Oracle.
